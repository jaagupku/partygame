# Attributions

## Sound effects

Most in-game sound effects are adapted from Kenney sound packs:

- Creator: Kenney
- Source: https://kenney.nl/
- License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
- License URL: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The Kenney-derived sound effects are renamed in the app to gameplay cues such as
`answer-reveal.wav`, `buzzer-armed.wav`, `buzzer.ogg`, `finale-reveal.wav`,
`finale-stage.ogg`, `pending-review-cleared.wav`, `scoreboard-shown.ogg`,
`step-open.ogg`, `step-closed.wav`, `submission-received.ogg`,
`timer-warning.wav`, and `winner-podium.ogg`.

## Legacy sound effects

The current frontend runtime still imports these legacy Freesound effects:
